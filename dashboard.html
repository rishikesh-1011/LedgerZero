🔥 FIELD 12: "WHAT BROKE, AND HOW YOU GOT OUT" (READ FIRST BY RAZORPAY)

1. What Broke: Boundary Precision & Over-eager Candidate Matching
Split-payment sums can cross a money boundary when compared as binary floats, and same-amount decoys can look plausible when a matcher ignores conflicting references. A benchmark that only shows a success percentage cannot expose either failure.

2. How We Got Out (The Architectural Pivot):
• Cent-normalized deterministic rules: exact, fuzzy, and split stages compare integer paise; fuzzy matching uses an explicit ±₹5.00 tolerance and split matching permits only two paise of aggregation drift.
• AI judgment guardrail: exact IDs and arithmetic never use an LLM. Stage 4 is optional, chunked, schema-constrained, and rejects any proposal with conflicting non-empty references.
• Proof instead of a claim: the live benchmark retains hidden truth only for scoring, reports precision/recall/collisions at runtime, and routes unresolved rows to a human review queue rather than force-matching them.

Problem Taste & Build Quality

Focused on verification capacity, not generation speed: multi-source parsing, reproducible hidden-truth evaluation, bounded uploads, and explicit failure responses.

AI Judgment & Closed-Loop Ops

The right tool in the right place: deterministic rules for money and references; optional local Qwen2.5-3B review for constrained ambiguity; policy-limited actions with a locally verifiable hash-chain audit record.

SYSTEM OVERVIEW & CORE DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

LedgerZero is an autonomous financial controller engineered to replace manual end-of-month close with real-time, mathematically deterministic verification paired with guarded local AI reasoning.

Instead of treating AI as an infallible black box for accounting arithmetic, LedgerZero enforces a strict separation of concerns: deterministic rule passes handle all money boundaries and exact UTRs, while an optional local LLM provides human-like judgment exclusively for semantic vendor discrepancies and unstructured invoices.

1. Multi-Format Ingestion & 5-Stage Matching

Natively ingests PDF, Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx), CSV, XML, and JSON statements. Passes records through Exact ID Matching, Normalized Fuzzy Text Matching (with ±₹5.00 tolerance), N:1 Split-Payment aggregation, and Guarded Heuristic / LLM Stage 4.

2. Settlement Q&A Agent

Context-grounded assistant that answers complex controller inquiries ("Why was invoice #104 split?", "Explain vendor variance for Acme"). Quotes exact ledger and bank citation lines with zero hallucination.

3. Cash Forecaster & Liquidity Engine

Projects 30-day liquidity trajectories using dynamic run-rates, vendor settlement cadences, and seasonal cash cycles. Flags cash-cliff alerts before liquidity risks materialize.

4. Statutory Tax & GST Matcher

Automatically audits Section 194C/J/H TDS withholdings and GST credit discrepancies across vendor payouts, flagging non-compliant tax deductions before regulatory filings.
